# Changelog

## 0.5.0 - 2026-10-07

- Explicit single-image PNG to JPEG comparison, conversion and exact restoration;
  retain originals until separately confirmed permanent deletion.
- Measured Web Q90, Good quality Q94 and Best quality Q98 choices. Recommend only
  the lightest passing profile; no passing profile means no automatic choice.
- Clear warning, comparison and independent server-enforced acknowledgement for
  non-validated manual choices; provenance approval remains separate.
- Fenced reconciliation of proven unstarted historical review claims, without
  encoding or rewriting history. No automatic format conversion or new schema.

## 0.5.0-private.5 - explicit manual quality choice

- Recommend only the lightest quality-passing profile. Quality failure alone
  no longer disables safe, beneficial manual choices; no passing default means
  no automatic selection.
- Open comparison and require a separate explicit quality acknowledgement for
  failing choices, enforced server-side and durably recorded with actual quality.
- Keep technical/benefit guards, SQL authority, restoration and provenance intact.
- Local-only candidate; no real attachment reconversion or deployment.

## 0.5.0-private.4 - measured per-image JPEG profiles

- Explicit PNG to JPEG radios: Web Q90, Good quality Q94, Best quality Q98.
- Actual measured sizes, smallest-passing default and disabled rejected choices;

- Durable selected-profile evidence, truthful multi-profile peak/temporary bytes
  and identity-safe cleanup. Native derivatives remain independently conservative.
- No automatic conversion, bulk expansion or change to ordinary JPEG/lossless PNG.

## 0.4.0 - private V1 candidate (not publicly released)

- Accepted M1-M5.1 analysis, immutable plans, fenced DB ownership, verified JPEG
